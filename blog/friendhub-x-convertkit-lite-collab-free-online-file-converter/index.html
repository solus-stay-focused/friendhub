Oct 1, 2026 3 min read
Collab

FriendHub and ConvertKit Lite are teaming up. FriendHub is where you meet and chat with people. ConvertKit Lite is where you convert the files you share along the way, for free.

Why FriendHub and ConvertKit Lite Together?

Both projects come from the same builder and share one idea: online tools should be simple, quick to open, and free to use. FriendHub covers live conversation. ConvertKit Lite covers the practical side of sharing files, such as a PDF that is too large, a photo in the wrong format, or a document you need as Word.

This post explains what ConvertKit Lite offers and how to find the right tool fast.

What Is ConvertKit Lite?

ConvertKit Lite is a free online file converter with 39 tools in one place. You can convert PDFs, Word and Excel files, images, audio, and video without creating an account. It works in any browser, on desktop or phone.

PDF & documents

PDF to Word, Word to PDF, PDF to Excel, PDF to PowerPoint, merge, split, and compress PDF files.

Images

JPG to PNG, PNG to JPG, HEIC to JPG, WebP conversion, image compressor and resizer, background remover.

Audio & video

MP4 to MP3, MP3 to WAV, MOV to MP4, and MP4 to GIF.

Data & utilities

XLSX to CSV, CSV to XLSX, ZIP and RAR extractor, QR code generator, age and BMI calculators.

In short

No registration, free to use, and it works on any device.

How to Convert a File in Four Steps

  1. Open ConvertKit Lite.
  2. Pick the tool you need from the list, or use the category filters (Image, PDF & Document, Audio, Video, Archive, Data).
  3. Add your file and start the conversion.
  4. Download the result and carry on with your work.

Popular Conversions People Look For

These are some of the most common tasks, all available from the free online file converter:

  • Merge PDF and Split PDF to combine or separate pages.
  • Compress PDF to make a file small enough to email or upload.
  • PDF to Word when you need to edit a document.
  • HEIC to JPG for iPhone photos that won't open elsewhere.
  • Image compressor and resizer for faster websites and smaller uploads.
  • MP4 to MP3 to pull the audio out of a video.

Who Is It For?

Students turning notes into PDFs, freelancers sending files to clients, designers preparing images for different platforms, and anyone who just needs one quick conversion without installing software or signing up.
