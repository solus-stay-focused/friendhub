Random chat can be entertaining, but it's important to remember that you're interacting with people you don't know. Follow a few basic online safety practices whenever you're using random video chat platforms.

  • Leave uncomfortable conversations. You don't have to continue a conversation that makes you uncomfortable.
  • Use reporting tools when necessary. If someone behaves inappropriately, use the platform's available safety and reporting features.
