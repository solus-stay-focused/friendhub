Why Omegle Disappeared

Omegle's founder cited the platform's association with misuse and the immense burden of content moderation as the core reasons for shutting down. It's a fair point — running an anonymous, unfiltered video chat platform at scale is genuinely hard. But the demand for random video chat never went away. People still want to meet strangers, practice languages, make new friends, or just have a spontaneous conversation with someone outside their usual social circle.

That demand is exactly why alternatives like FriendHub exist — but built with a modern approach to safety and moderation from day one, rather than as an afterthought.

Staying Safe on Any Random Video Chat Platform

Whether you're using FriendHub or any similar site, a few habits go a long way toward keeping the experience positive:

  • Keep personal details private. Never share your full name, home address, phone number, or financial information with a stranger, no matter how friendly the conversation feels.
  • Stick to the platform. Be cautious if someone quickly tries to move the conversation to another app — this is a common tactic used by people with bad intentions.
  • Report anything that feels off. You don't need a perfect explanation. If a conversation makes you uncomfortable, end it and report it.
  • Trust your instincts. You can leave any chat instantly, for any reason, with zero explanation required.
